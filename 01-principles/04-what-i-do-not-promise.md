# What I deliberately do not promise

Clear boundaries create trust.

## I do not promise

- "mission-critical" outcomes for environments that are not built and governed as such
- fictional failover narratives ("everything fails over to everything")
- compliance certification via technical artefacts

## I do promise

- explicit design choices and accepted risks
- clear operating boundaries and handover quality
- evidence-oriented delivery outputs that support reviews and audits

## Why this page exists

In regulated environments, overpromising is operational debt.
The goal is stable, reviewable services with clear ownership.
