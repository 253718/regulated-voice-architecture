# Evidence is not compliance

A recurring confusion in regulated projects:

- **Evidence**: factual artefacts and traceability.
- **Compliance**: governance, controls, policies, and sign-off.

## Principle

Evidence packs should be:
- factual (no claims)
- read-only (no hidden changes)
- integrity-protected (manifests, checksums)

They help:
- audits
- handovers
- architecture reviews

They do not certify compliance.
