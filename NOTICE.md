# Notice (Anonymization & Boundaries)

This repository is designed to be safe to share publicly.

## What is included

- Narrative case studies built from real-world work patterns.
- Architecture principles and decision rationales.
- Generic diagrams and checklists.

## What is intentionally excluded

- Client names, identifiers, phone numbers, SIP trunks, tenant domains.
- Email excerpts, meeting transcripts, internal ticket details.
- Tenant-changing runbooks and operational credentials.

## Operator and product references

Operator and vendor names may appear **only** when they are essential to explain the responsibility boundary or the architectural model.

## If you spot sensitive material

Please open an issue (or contact the author privately) describing the file and the section to redact.
