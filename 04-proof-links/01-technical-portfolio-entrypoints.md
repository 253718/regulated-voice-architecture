## How to use proof

- Use this repo to understand *decisions* and *operating assumptions*.
- Use the technical repo to validate the *delivery discipline* and *evidence quality*.
- Use the DAT template to understand what a clean as-built architecture handover should contain.
- Use the DEX template to understand what support needs in order to operate the service after BUILD handover.
